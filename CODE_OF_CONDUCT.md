# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Treat everyone with respect. Welcome questions, assume good intent, give specific and constructive feedback, and make room for learners with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [kodenvibe@gmail.com](mailto:kodenvibe@gmail.com). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.
